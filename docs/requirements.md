The final-output drain ends as soon as UART and OLED are idle. `APP_SHUTDOWN_DRAIN_MS` sets a 600 ms loop budget for serialized UART chunks and the OLED frame, with a deadline check between their work. One bounded UART or OLED call already in progress can finish slightly beyond that budget. It does not perform new sensor acquisition, input handling or game updates. `External_FinishDisplay` advances only the pending OLED frame. UART idle requires both an empty queue and the hardware transmission-complete flag.

The laptop terminal flag is published inside the final frame's sequence lock. After accepting that coherent frame, the viewer closes its owned OpenOCD/ST-Link connection and stops debugger polling; it retains the final image and requires board reset plus an explicit reconnect. This closes the viewer's communication session without claiming that the MCU's external debug hardware becomes electrically inaccessible. Record application UART silence directly in the physical termination demonstration.

The extra game round result is distinct from compulsory termination. Victory or ordinary game defeat leaves battle operating; the rescue-timeout terminal state stops operation until reset. Missing optional OLED/IR/buzzer modules do not replace or disable the six compulsory sensors.

## Sensor conversions and freshness

| Quantity | Device and configuration | Conversion used | Acquisition/failure handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | HTS221, 7 Hz conversion | Linear interpolation between factory `T0/T1` calibration endpoints, in °C | Checked calibration, control/status and I2C reads; invalid data is unavailable |
| Relative humidity | HTS221, 7 Hz conversion | Linear interpolation between factory `H0/H1` endpoints, in %RH | Shared four-byte humidity/temperature read; checked data-ready flags |
| Pressure | LPS22HB, 25 Hz conversion | Signed 24-bit output / 4096, in hPa | Auto-increment and block-data update; fresh conversion required |
| Acceleration | LSM6DSL, ±2 g, 52 Hz conversion | Signed 16-bit × 0.061 mg/LSB × 0.00980665 m/s² per mg | Read every 50 ms and during the one-second sweep; repeated data accepted for at most one 50 ms interval |
| Angular rate | LSM6DSL, ±2000 °/s, 52 Hz conversion | Signed 16-bit × 70 millidegrees/s per LSB / 1000, in °/s | Validity and freshness tracked independently of acceleration |
| Magnetic field | LIS3MDL, ±4 gauss, 40 Hz conversion | Bundled BSP scale: signed 16-bit × 0.14 milligauss/LSB × 0.1 µT per milligauss | All three axes, fresh conversion required; scale is the BSP approximation |

The UART prints all three axes with units for A/G/M when no threshold is violated. Thresholds for these vectors use Euclidean magnitude, `sqrt(x²+y²+z²)`. A failed read clears its validity and exposes unavailable data rather than a fabricated zero. Runtime I2C transactions use a 5 ms HAL timeout and reject a pre-existing BUSY flag before entering HAL's separate busy wait. Initialized sensor health can be inspected through `Board_SensorHealth`; the first connected-board snapshot confirmed valid converted values after conversion began, but did not calibrate their accuracy.

## Starting thresholds and calibration

These are engineering starting values, not measured laboratory limits or universal safety limits. Values exactly on a boundary are accepted. Tune `Core/Inc/app_config.h`, then rebuild and record the change and its measured reason.

| Sensor | Accepted range | Initial reasoning and calibration task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | 10–45 °C | Broad indoor operating envelope. Compare room baseline against a thermometer; choose a practical abnormal threshold for the demonstration. |
| Pressure | 850–1100 hPa | Broad near-ground atmospheric band. Record local baseline and any systematic sensor difference. This reports pressure; it does not claim a calibrated altitude estimate. |
| Humidity | 20–85 %RH | Broad comfort/operating envelope. Compare against a reference meter where available and record room variation. |
| Acceleration magnitude | 0–30 m/s² | Approximately 3 g marks a large disturbance relative to the ~9.81 m/s² resting magnitude. A ±2 g sensor can clip; this is not a calibrated impact recorder. |
| Angular-rate magnitude | 0–250 °/s | Indicates rapid rotation. A smooth flip can trigger an attack without necessarily exceeding this independent warning threshold. |
| Magnetic-field magnitude | 0–200 µT | Separates a broad normal Earth-field range from a nearby strong magnetic disturbance; local metal and electronics require a measured baseline. |

For each sensor, record at least a quiet baseline, expected handling variation, chosen threshold, the resulting warning log and recovery. Do not heat, wet or impact the board merely to force a threshold. A clearly declared debugger-injected demonstration can show logic when environmental manipulation is impractical; see the [demo checklist](demo-checklist.md).

Orientation uses the first valid, slow-moving sample as the intended upright reference. Accepted acceleration magnitude is 6–13 m/s²; initial gyro magnitude must be at most 20 °/s. A reference-angle inversion of roughly 135° or more must persist for 350 ms and be corroborated by at least 60° of accumulated gyro motion after leaving the upright region. A >250 ms sample gap breaks qualification. A gyro measures angular rate, so acceleration supplies absolute gravity orientation. A smooth 0.5–2 s flip avoids missing the motion between 50 ms samples. After rescue the board must return upright before attack detection rearms.

## Enhancements and reporting

Implemented enhancements are generated human/zombie targets, scoring and lives, IR aim bonus, original Garden Guard pixel animation, plant/target/projectile/impact sprites, an OLED score/hearts/energy HUD, a native laptop viewer of the MCU's actual framebuffer, buzzer sequences, a rescue countdown, queued UART with bounded foreground polling, input race handling and optional-module fault isolation. The reflective sensor only detects object presence; red/green identity comes from the game. Optional TCS34725 readings can warn about a disagreement but do not alter scoring or compulsory firing.
